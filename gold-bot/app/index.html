How it decides. BUY when a 4-hour candle closes above the highest high of the previous 55 candles, while price is above the 200 EMA. The first stop loss is 2 × ATR below entry. After that it becomes a trailing stop: it follows the highest price minus 3 × ATR and only ever moves up. CLOSE when price touches the stop. There is no fixed take profit, so winners can run. It never says SELL: selling gold lost money in testing. Price is PAXG, a token backed by 1 oz of gold, streamed live; use "Match my broker" so the numbers line up with your MT5. Not financial advice.
