## Tips
- Record somewhere quiet, like a closet or under a blanket, with the phone mic 15–20 cm from your mouth.
- If a line runs long, just drop the word "actually" or "so". The cuts don't need to match word-for-word.
- In CapCut, keep the video sound around 60–80% and your voice at 100%. The music is already mixed low so your voice can sit on top.
- If you send me the CapCut export, I'll clean up the voice and re-balance it, like I did for the launch reel.
