# Where to promote the pre-order, and how

## Which social media to use (ranked)

| # | Platform | Why | Bots |
|---|----------|-----|------|
| 1 | **Instagram** | Clothes sell on looks. Reels + Stories + DMs = the most pre-orders. | 2 DM bots (ManyChat) + site link in bio |
| 2 | **TikTok** | Biggest reach to people who don't know you yet. | 2 DM bots if ManyChat TikTok works for you, otherwise auto-reply + site link |
| 3 | **Facebook** (Page + Marketplace + groups) | Local buyers, family, community groups. | 2 Messenger bots + site link |
| 4 | **Snapchat** | Friends and close followers. Stories convert well. | No DM bots. Put the site link on Stories (link sticker) |
| 5 | **WhatsApp** | People who already have your number. | Greeting + quick replies, or full ManyChat bots |
| 6 | **YouTube Shorts / X** | Extra reach if you already post there. | Site link in bio/description |

**Answer: 6 places**, but don't do them all on day 1. Start with **Instagram + TikTok**, then add Facebook and Snapchat once those are running. Every platform points to the same website, and the website has both bots, so no buyer is ever left without someone to chat with.

## 2-week launch plan

| Day | Post | Where |
|-----|------|-------|
| 1 | **Teaser:** close-up of the fabric/print, no full reveal. "Dropping something. Comment 🔥 if you want first dibs." | IG Reel, TikTok, Snap story |
| 2 | **Story poll:** "Black or White?" | IG + Snap stories |
| 3 | **Full reveal:** shirt on a person, slow-mo, music. "Pre-order now. Comment SHIRT 👇" | All |
| 4 | **Behind the scenes:** designing it, printing it, packing. | TikTok, IG story |
| 5 | **Fit check:** 2-3 friends with different body types in different sizes. Answers the #1 question. | Reel/TikTok |
| 6 | **Price + how to order** in 10 seconds: "Comment SHIRT → bot DMs you → done." | All |
| 7 | **First orders:** "X people already locked theirs in 🔥" (real numbers from the admin page). | Stories |
| 8 | **This or That:** "Black or White? Wrong answers only." | Reel/TikTok |
| 9 | **Repost** buyers' DMs/comments (with permission). | Stories |
| 10 | **Day in the life** wearing it. | TikTok |
| 11 | **"How I made it"** carousel. | IG post, FB |
| 12 | **48 hours left** countdown sticker. | All stories |
| 13 | **Last call** + reminder DM to everyone who hasn't ordered (see `dm-scripts/bot-scripts.md`). | All |
| 14 | **Closed. Thank you.** "Next drop coming 👀" builds the list for drop 2. | All |

## Caption templates
- "Pre-order the [Shirt Name] 👕 $[price]. Comment **SHIRT** and my bot will DM you everything."
- "Only taking pre-orders until [deadline]. Once it's gone, it's gone. Link in bio 🔗"
- "Black or White? 👇 Comment SHIRT to lock yours."

## Reuse what you already have
The reels in `../edited/` (the This-or-That runner reel and the sprint edits) already use the format that works. Keep the same style: fast cuts, music, and SFX on each cut. Swap in shirt shots and end every video with "Comment SHIRT 👇".

## Tips that matter
- **Comment keyword in every post.** That's what fires the DM bot.
- **Post at the same time every day.** Evenings (6-9pm) usually work best for this kind of audience.
- **Check the admin page daily** (`/admin`). Mark payments, and use the size totals to know what to print.
- **Never cold-DM people** or buy followers. Platforms will limit your account.
