# TikTok drafts: launch week

Seven ready-to-film TikToks for the first week of the pre-order. Each is 7-15 seconds and can be shot on a phone.
Swap `[brackets]` for your real details. After this week, use **🎵 TikTok → Post Writer** in `/admin` for fresh drafts. It uses your real deadline and order count.

**Every video:** put the call to action ("comment SHIRT 👇") in the last 2 seconds, and pin a comment: "Pre-order link in bio 🔗"

---

## Day 1: Teaser
**Hook (0-2s):** Hand slowly pulls something out of a bag. Text: *"3 months of work. 7 seconds."*

| Time | Film | Text on screen |
|------|------|----------------|
| 0-2s | Hand pulling the shirt halfway out of a bag | 3 months of work. 7 seconds. |
| 2-5s | Super close-up of the print/fabric, slow pan | you're not ready |
| 5-7s | Shirt yanked back into the bag | dropping soon 👀 |

**Sound:** suspense build-up that cuts off · **Post:** 7pm
**Caption:** something's coming. comment 🔥 if you want first dibs
**Hashtags:** #streetwear #clothingbrand #smallbusiness #comingsoon
