# Instagram setup (best platform for this)

**What you need:** an Instagram **Professional account** (Creator or Business, free; switch it in Settings) and a free ManyChat account at manychat.com.

## Connect
1. ManyChat → **Sign up with Instagram**, pick your account, and allow message access.
2. In the Instagram app: Settings → Messages and story replies → Message controls → **Connected tools → Allow access to messages** ON.

## Bot 1: Shirt Helper triggers (make all 3)
Build the **Shirt Helper** welcome + button replies from `bot-scripts.md`, then attach these triggers:

1. **Comment trigger** (the big one): Automation → New → "User comments on your post or reel".
   - Keyword: `SHIRT` (also add `shirt`, `I want one`, `🔥`).
   - Public reply under the comment: "Check your DMs 👀" (rotate 3 versions so it doesn't look spammy).
   - Then send the DM welcome message.
2. **DM keyword trigger:** someone DMs "shirt", "price", "size", or "preorder" → welcome message.
3. **Story reply trigger:** someone replies to your story or reacts with 🔥 → welcome message.

## Bot 2: Order Taker
Build the **Order Taker** flow from `bot-scripts.md`. Start it from the `🛒 I want one` button. Also add a DM keyword trigger: `order`.

## Where to put the link
- **Bio:** your website link (it has both bots on it).
- **Every post caption ends with:** "Comment SHIRT and I'll DM you the pre-order link 👇"
- **Stories:** use the link sticker, plus a poll ("Black or White?"). Poll voters are your warm buyers.

## Instagram rules to stay safe
- The auto-DM only goes to people who messaged or commented first. Never cold-DM strangers.
- You have 24 hours after their last message to reply automatically.
