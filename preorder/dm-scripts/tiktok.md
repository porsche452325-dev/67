# TikTok setup (best reach for new people)

**What you need:** a TikTok **Business account** (free; switch it in Settings → Account) and ManyChat with its TikTok integration.
> TikTok DM automation is newer and its availability depends on your country and account. If ManyChat won't let you connect TikTok, skip to "No-bot fallback" below. It still works great.

## Connect
ManyChat → Settings → Channels → **TikTok → Connect**, then log in and approve.

## Bot 1: Shirt Helper
- **Comment trigger:** keyword `SHIRT` on any video → auto-DM the Shirt Helper welcome from `bot-scripts.md`.
- **DM keyword trigger:** "shirt", "price", "size" → welcome message.

## Bot 2: Order Taker
Same flow as `bot-scripts.md`, started by the `🛒 I want one` button or the DM keyword `order`.

## No-bot fallback (works for everyone)
- Put the website link in your bio. You need a Business account to get a clickable link.
- Pin a comment on every video: "Pre-order link in bio 🔗 the bot answers 24/7"
- Turn on **auto-replies** in TikTok (Settings → Business suite → Direct messages → Automatic message) with:
  > Yo thanks for the message! 🔥 Pre-order the [Shirt Name] here, my bot will answer anything and take your order: [your-site-link]

## What to post
See `../GROWTH.md`. Keep the "comment SHIRT" call to action in the last 2 seconds of every video.
