# Facebook setup (local buyers, groups, family and friends)

**What you need:** a Facebook **Page** for your brand (not your personal profile) and ManyChat.

## Connect
ManyChat → Settings → Channels → **Facebook Messenger → Connect**, then pick your Page.
If your Instagram is linked to the Page, both can live in the same ManyChat account.

## Bot 1: Shirt Helper
- **Comment trigger** on Page posts: keyword `SHIRT` → public reply "Sent you a message!" + Messenger welcome from `bot-scripts.md`.
- **Default reply / welcome message:** anyone who opens Messenger with your Page gets the welcome.
- **Ice breakers** (the question buttons people see before typing): "📏 Sizes?", "🚚 Shipping?", "🛒 Pre-order".

## Bot 2: Order Taker
Same flow as `bot-scripts.md`, started from `🛒 I want one` / "🛒 Pre-order".

## Getting seen on Facebook
- Share the Page post to your **personal profile** and to local **buy/sell groups**. Check each group's rules first.
- **Marketplace:** list "[Shirt Name] PRE-ORDER" with the price and photos. In the description: "Message me to pre-order, my bot replies instantly."
  Marketplace chats come from your personal profile and can't be automated. Paste the website link as your reply.
