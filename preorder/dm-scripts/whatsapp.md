# WhatsApp setup (for people who already have your number)

Good for friends, family, school, and local buyers who'd rather text.

## Free option: WhatsApp Business app (no bot, but close)
1. Install **WhatsApp Business** (free) on your phone.
2. Business tools → **Greeting message:**
   > Yo! 👋 Want the [Shirt Name]? It's $[price], pre-order closes [deadline].
   > Ask me anything, or order in 1 minute with my bot: [your-site-link]
3. Business tools → **Quick replies:** save `/sizes`, `/ship`, `/pay`, `/order` with the texts from `bot-scripts.md`. Type `/` to send them in one tap.
4. Business tools → **Catalog:** add the shirt with photos and price.
5. Post on your **Status** every couple of days during the pre-order.

## Full bot option: ManyChat + WhatsApp
ManyChat → Channels → **WhatsApp → Connect**. This needs a phone number that isn't already on regular WhatsApp, and WhatsApp charges small per-conversation fees.
Then build the same two bots from `bot-scripts.md`, with the keyword trigger `shirt`.
